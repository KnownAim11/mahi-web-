Advanced Skincare FAQ

Is the Buccal Facial massage painful?

Not at all. While the technique involves intra-oral massage to release deep muscle tension, it is highly therapeutic and relaxing. Most clients find the sculpting effect very soothing.

Is there any downtime after the treatment?

No. Our Korean techniques focus on barrier repair and gentle transformation. You will leave with a "Glass Skin" glow and can return to your daily activities immediately.

How often should I book an Advanced Skincare session?

For optimal results, particularly with Lymphatic Drainage and Peptide infusions, we recommend a session every 4 to 6 weeks to maintain skin health and muscle tone.

Are these treatments safe for sensitive skin?

Absolutely. Mahi's 20+ years of experience allows her to adapt every ritual for even the most reactive skin types using hypoallergenic, medical-grade Korean products.
