Frequently Asked Questions

How often should I get a Korean Head Spa treatment?

For optimal results, we recommend a Korean Head Spa treatment every 4-6 weeks. This allows your scalp to maintain its health and your hair to continue growing strong and healthy.

Is the treatment suitable for all hair types?

Yes, our Korean Head Spa treatment is suitable for all hair types and textures. The treatment is customized based on your scalp analysis to address your specific needs.

Will my hair feel greasy after the treatment?

No, your hair will feel clean, light, and refreshed. The treatment removes excess oil and build-up, leaving your scalp and hair feeling breathable and revitalized.

Can I style my hair after the treatment?

Yes, you can style your hair immediately after the treatment. Your hair will be clean and ready for styling, with enhanced volume and shine from the deep cleansing and conditioning.
